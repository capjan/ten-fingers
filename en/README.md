## Made for your Mac

A native macOS app with light and dark appearances. Use your keyboard, mouse, or trackpad, and adjust the text size to suit you.

Your training data is stored locally. There is no account to create and no advertising to interrupt your practice. [Read about app privacy](privacy-policy/privacy-policy-in-the-macos-app-ten-typing-fingers/README.md).

## Ready for your first lesson?

Begin with the home row, or choose a lesson for the keys you want to type more confidently.
