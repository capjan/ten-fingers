# How can I help?

Have a question about Ten Typing Fingers, a problem during practice, or an idea for the app? Email [support@tenfingers.app](mailto:support@tenfingers.app).

## Report a problem

These details help me understand what happened:

- Your app version and macOS version.
- What you wanted to do and what happened instead.
- The steps needed to reproduce the problem.
- If useful, a screenshot with personal information removed.

## Ideas and public feedback

You can also share bug reports and feature requests through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/capjan/ten-fingers/issues). Posts are public. Please send personal information or purchase details by email only.
