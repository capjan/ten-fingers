# Privacy Policy in the macOS App Ten Typing Fingers

We take data protection and privacy seriously! In our macOS app Ten Typing Fingers, we do not collect any personal information, either directly or indirectly.

## Data collection and usage

The macOS app Ten Typing Fingers does not collect any personal data such as names, mail addresses, location data or other personal information.

## Tracking

The Ten Typing Fingers app does not use any tracking technologies to record user activities.

## Disclosure of data to third parties

As we do not collect any personal data, there is nothing that could be passed on.

## Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns about our privacy practices, please contact us at [privacy@tenfingers.app](mailto:privacy@tenfingers.app).

## Changes to this privacy policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to our privacy practices. The current version is available on this page.
